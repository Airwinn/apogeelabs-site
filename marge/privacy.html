Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 October 2026. Questions: hello@apogeelabs.org.

Marge (“the app”, “we”) is a profit and ROI tracker for resellers. We built it to be private by default: your inventory lives on your device, signing in is optional, and we do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

This policy explains exactly what data is processed, when, and by whom.

1. Local-first storage

Marge stores your flips, sales, photos and settings in a database on your device. The app is fully usable with no account at all. If you never sign in, we have no copy of your inventory on a server, and deleting the app deletes your data.

1a. Optional account & sync

If you want your data on more than one device, you can create an account with an email and password, or with Sign in with Apple or Google. This is entirely optional. When you are signed in:

  • We store your email address (or the identifier Apple/Google returns) to operate your account. With Sign in with Apple you may use Apple’s “Hide My Email” relay.
  • We may email you about your account, for example service problems or changes to your plan; you can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any such email or writing to hello@apogeelabs.org.
  • Your flips and sales are mirrored to our database (hosted by Supabase) so they can sync to your other devices. Each row is tied to your account and protected by row-level security, so only you can read it.
  • Item photos stay on each device and are not uploaded.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and the flips and sales stored on our server. A few code, invite and purchase records stay (section 6). Data already synced to your other devices is removed from those devices on their next sync; local data on the current device follows the app’s normal delete/uninstall behaviour.

2. Data processed when you use AI / lookup features

A few optional features send a limited amount of data to our servers, which forward it to the third-party providers below to do the work, and return the result. We do not store this content.

FeatureWhat is sentSent toWhy
Scan a buy with AI The photo(s) you choose of the item and/or receipt Our server → Google Gemini To read the item name, price, date and other details to pre-fill the form
Smart-map an import Your spreadsheet’s column headers and a few sample rows Our server → Google Gemini To match your columns to the right fields
Scan a barcode The scanned barcode number (UPC/EAN) Our server → UPCitemdb To look up the product name and image

For abuse and cost protection, our server temporarily processes the network IP address of the request to enforce a per-day usage limit. We retain only a per-day request count; we do not build a profile from it.

Photos and receipts can contain personal information. Only send images you are comfortable sharing for processing. These features are optional; the app is fully usable without them.

2a. Promo codes and invites

Typing a promo code and the Invite friends screen need an account. A free month is one per person, so our server checks that each request comes from the real Marge app on a real phone. For these two features only, the app sends our server:

  • On iPhone: an App Attest key that Apple confirms Marge made on this phone, and a DeviceCheck token. With that token our server reads or sets two yes/no flags that Apple keeps for this phone, such as "this phone already had a free month". The app also sends the ID Apple gives Marge on this phone (identifierForVendor); the server checks its format and does not keep it.
  • On Android: the Android ID, with a Play Integrity token that covers it. Google reads the token for us and says whether the app and the phone are genuine. Google can also keep a few yes/no flags for this phone, which we use the same way.

The server never stores these IDs as they are. It keeps only a peppered hash: a one-way code made with a secret key, which cannot be turned back into the ID or matched with other apps. It also keeps a peppered hash of your email address (Gmail dots and +tags removed), so one mailbox cannot take a second free month with a new account. Each code and invite request is logged with your account, the hashed phone ID, a peppered hash of the network IP address, the code and the result.

Invites. Your account gets a referral code and a link (apogeelabs.org/marge/r/ and your code). You share it yourself; Marge never reads your contacts. When a friend uses it, we record which account invited which, and whether the friend's free month and first payment happened, so we can give you your free month. Your invite list shows numbers only, never a friend's name or email.

We use this data only to give free months and rewards and to stop abuse. It is never used for ads or tracking, and we never sell it.

2b. Subscriptions

You buy Pro or Expert through Apple or Google; we never see your card. RevenueCat manages subscription status for us. When you are signed in, RevenueCat and our server receive your purchase events (product, price, store, dates and transaction IDs) with your account ID. We use them to unlock your plan on all your devices, to apply free months and invite rewards, and to handle refunds.

3. Camera and photo library

With your permission, the app uses the camera and photo library so you can attach a photo to a flip or scan an item/receipt/barcode. Photos stay on your device unless you use an AI feature above. Marge does not use your phone's location; a location permission string appears only because a bundled file-picker component references it, and the app never requests or reads your location. (Google Analytics estimates a rough region from the network address; see section 5.)

4. What we do NOT collect

  • No name or profile beyond the email/identifier used to sign in (and only if you choose to).
  • No advertising identifiers, no ad networks, no cross-app tracking.
  • No profiling. Usage counts and crash reports (section 5) use app IDs, never your account or email.
  • We never sell or rent your data.

5. Third-party processors

  • Google Gemini: processes images/headers you submit to AI features. Google Gemini Privacy Policy. API inputs are not used to train their models.
  • UPCitemdb: receives a scanned barcode to return product info.
  • Supabase: hosts the server functions that broker the requests above and, if you sign in, stores your account and synced flips/sales. It also stores the code, invite and purchase records in sections 2a and 2b, and our own usage counter: a random install ID and step names such as "onboarding finished", never an item, an amount or your email. Supabase Privacy Policy.
  • Apple / Google: only if you choose “Sign in with Apple” or Google, they authenticate you and return an email or identifier. When you use a code or an invite, Apple (App Attest, DeviceCheck) or Google (Play Integrity) confirms that the app and the phone are genuine (section 2a).
  • RevenueCat: manages subscriptions and receives your purchase events and, if you are signed in, your account ID. RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: counts app usage, such as screens opened and "item added" or "sale logged" with a category, quantity and amount, never an item name. It uses app IDs, not your account, and estimates a rough region from the network address. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Sentry: receives crash and error reports (the error, device model and system version) so we can fix bugs. No account ID is attached. Sentry Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention

Your inventory: kept on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. If you sign in, your synced flips/sales are kept on our server until you delete them or delete your account. AI/lookup content: not stored by us after the request completes. Rate-limit counters: a per-IP, per-day count that resets daily.

Code and invite records (the peppered hashes of your phone ID, email and IP address, invite records and the request log) and purchase records stay after you delete your account. Their job is to stop the same person or phone from taking a second free month and to handle refunds. They hold no plain email and no inventory.

7. Your rights

You control your data directly: edit or delete any flip or sale in the app, export to CSV at any time, and remove local data by deleting the app. If you created an account, you can delete it and its server-side data from Settings → Account → Delete account. If you are in the EEA/UK, you have rights under the GDPR; for any access or deletion request you can also contact us below.

8. Children

Marge is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

9. Security

Requests to our servers use HTTPS. Secrets (such as the Google Gemini key) live only on the server, never in the app. No method of transmission is 100% secure, but we keep the data we touch minimal and transient.

10. Changes

We may update this policy; we will revise the “Last updated” date and, for material changes, note it in the app or release notes.

11. Contact
